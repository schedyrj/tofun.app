<section id="collect"><h2>1. Information we collect</h2>
<h3>Information you provide</h3><ul><li>Account information, such as your name, phone number, email address, profile image, and login credentials.</li><li>Booking information, including selected services, appointment dates and times, notes, preferences, and booking history.</li><li>Communications you send to Tofun or participating service providers, including support requests and feedback.</li><li>Payment-related information. Where payments are available, card details may be processed directly by an authorized payment provider; Tofun may receive transaction status, amount, currency, and limited payment identifiers rather than full card details.</li></ul>
<h3>Information collected automatically</h3><ul><li>Device and technical information, such as device type, operating system, app version, language, IP address, and security or diagnostic data.</li><li>Usage information, such as screens viewed, features used, booking actions, timestamps, and crash or performance information.</li><li>Approximate or precise location only if a location feature is offered and you grant the relevant device permission. Location may be used to show nearby service providers.</li><li>Push-notification tokens if you enable notifications.</li></ul>
<h3>Information from service providers</h3><p>Participating salons, professionals, payment providers, authentication providers, and support partners may provide information needed to confirm or manage your booking, payment, or account.</p>
</section>

<section id="use"><h2>2. How we use information</h2><p>We may use information to:</p><ul><li>create and secure your account;</li><li>display relevant professionals and services;</li><li>create, confirm, change, and cancel appointments;</li><li>process or confirm payments and refunds where applicable;</li><li>send service messages, reminders, and requested notifications;</li><li>provide support and respond to requests;</li><li>detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and technical problems;</li><li>analyze and improve the reliability and usability of Tofun; and</li><li>comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.</li></ul><p>Where required, we rely on your consent. In other cases, processing may be necessary to provide the service, comply with law, or pursue legitimate interests such as security and service improvement.</p></section>

<section id="share"><h2>3. How we share information</h2><p>We may share information:</p><ul><li><strong>With service providers:</strong> booking details and necessary contact information are shared with the salon or professional you select.</li><li><strong>With processors:</strong> hosting, messaging, analytics, customer-support, identity, fraud-prevention, and payment partners may process information on our behalf.</li><li><strong>For legal and safety reasons:</strong> when reasonably necessary to comply with law, lawful requests, protect rights and safety, or investigate fraud and abuse.</li><li><strong>During a business transaction:</strong> in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.</li></ul><p>We do not sell personal information for money. If Tofun later introduces advertising or data practices considered “sale”, “sharing”, or cross-app tracking under applicable law, this policy and the relevant consent controls will be updated before that processing begins.</p></section>

<section id="retention"><h2>4. Retention</h2><p>We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Tofun, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, protect the service, and meet legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements. Retention periods vary by data type and purpose. When information is no longer required, we delete or anonymize it where reasonably possible.</p></section>
