Privacy Policy

Effective: 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tofun (“Tofun”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, shares, stores, and protects information when you use the Tofun mobile application, website, and related services.

Important before publication: This policy assumes Tofun uses the data categories described below. Remove any category the released app does not collect and add every SDK, payment provider, analytics service, or other data practice actually used. 1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information, such as your name, phone number, email address, profile image, and login credentials.
  • Booking information, including selected services, appointment dates and times, notes, preferences, and booking history.
  • Communications you send to Tofun or participating service providers, including support requests and feedback.
  • Payment-related information. Where payments are available, card details may be processed directly by an authorized payment provider; Tofun may receive transaction status, amount, currency, and limited payment identifiers rather than full card details.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical information, such as device type, operating system, app version, language, IP address, and security or diagnostic data.
  • Usage information, such as screens viewed, features used, booking actions, timestamps, and crash or performance information.
  • Approximate or precise location only if a location feature is offered and you grant the relevant device permission. Location may be used to show nearby service providers.
  • Push-notification tokens if you enable notifications.

Information from service providers

Participating salons, professionals, payment providers, authentication providers, and support partners may provide information needed to confirm or manage your booking, payment, or account.

2. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • create and secure your account;
  • display relevant professionals and services;
  • create, confirm, change, and cancel appointments;
  • process or confirm payments and refunds where applicable;
  • send service messages, reminders, and requested notifications;
  • provide support and respond to requests;
  • detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and technical problems;
  • analyze and improve the reliability and usability of Tofun; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

Where required, we rely on your consent. In other cases, processing may be necessary to provide the service, comply with law, or pursue legitimate interests such as security and service improvement.

3. How we share information

We may share information:

  • With service providers: booking details and necessary contact information are shared with the salon or professional you select.
  • With processors: hosting, messaging, analytics, customer-support, identity, fraud-prevention, and payment partners may process information on our behalf.
  • For legal and safety reasons: when reasonably necessary to comply with law, lawful requests, protect rights and safety, or investigate fraud and abuse.
  • During a business transaction: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal information for money. If Tofun later introduces advertising or data practices considered “sale”, “sharing”, or cross-app tracking under applicable law, this policy and the relevant consent controls will be updated before that processing begins.

4. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Tofun, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, protect the service, and meet legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements. Retention periods vary by data type and purpose. When information is no longer required, we delete or anonymize it where reasonably possible.

5. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to certain processing. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Account deletion

You may request deletion inside the app if that feature is available, or by emailing [email protected] with the subject “Tofun account deletion request.” We may verify your identity before acting. Deleting an account removes or anonymizes associated personal information unless retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, or transaction-record purposes.

You can manage location, camera, photo, and notification permissions through your device settings. You may unsubscribe from optional marketing messages using the method provided in the message.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No transmission or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

Tofun is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

8. International transfers

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers and require service providers to protect information consistently with applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Tofun, applicable law, or our data practices change. We will publish the revised policy with a new effective date and provide additional notice where required.
